HOW TO USE THIS DIRECTORY

One industry.
Many different workflows.

Travel & Tourism is not one job category. A travel agency, airline, hotel, destination management company and attraction operator can have different teams, systems and software. Use this page to break a broad industry into distinct freelance work areas, then check the employer's actual technology stack before proposing a service.

01 / DISTRIBUTION⌕

Travel agencies, OTAs & booking services

Help customers discover, compare, package and book flights, stays, activities and multi-part trips. This area spans consumer-facing booking sites, travel advisors and business travel services.

Freelance roles & services

  • Travel consultant and itinerary planner
  • Booking operations and reservation support
  • GDS booking support and ticketing specialist
  • OTA listing manager and inventory coordinator
  • Travel product researcher and package builder
  • Booking website UX / conversion specialist

Typical deliverables

  • Quoted itineraries and trip comparisons
  • Reservation data checks and booking SOPs
  • Supplier, fare and policy comparison sheets
  • Booking funnel audits and help content
Amadeus ↗Sabre ↗Travelport ↗Travefy ↗
02 / AIR TRANSPORT✈

Airlines & air travel services

Air travel workflows cover passenger reservations, fares, ticketing, schedule changes, disruption handling, loyalty, airline retailing and airport passenger information.

Freelance roles & services

  • Air ticketing and fare-rule support
  • Schedule change and disruption desk support
  • Airline content and route researcher
  • Airline website content / UX specialist
  • Passenger service knowledge-base writer
  • Travel data analyst and reporting specialist

Typical deliverables

  • Fare and route comparison reports
  • Passenger communication templates
  • Ticketing process documentation
  • Schedule and service information updates
Amadeus ↗Sabre ↗Travelport ↗

Airline reservation, ticketing and settlement access may require employer authorization, training and role-specific credentials.

03 / HOSPITALITY⌂

Hotels, resorts & alternative accommodation

Accommodation operations coordinate room inventory, rates, reservations, housekeeping, front desk, guest communications, distribution channels and property performance.

Freelance roles & services

  • Hotel content and OTA listing specialist
  • Revenue / rate research analyst
  • Property management system (PMS) support
  • Channel manager and inventory coordinator
  • Guest communication and pre-arrival specialist
  • Hotel website and direct-booking UX designer

Typical deliverables

  • Room descriptions, amenities and image audits
  • Rate-shop reports and competitor sets
  • Guest messaging templates and SOPs
  • Channel mapping and booking journey reviews
Cloudbeds ↗SiteMinder ↗Mews ↗Little Hotelier ↗
04 / EXPERIENCES⌖

Tour operators, activities & destination management (DMC)

Operators package and deliver guided tours, excursions, multi-day trips, local experiences and destination services. Work spans product design, supplier coordination, scheduling, availability and on-trip logistics.

Freelance roles & services

  • Tour product and itinerary designer
  • Destination management coordinator
  • Tour operations and supplier coordinator
  • Activity inventory and booking-system manager
  • Guide scheduling and logistics assistant
  • Travel experience copywriter

Typical deliverables

  • Day-by-day itineraries and inclusions
  • Supplier rates, contracts and operations sheets
  • Activity listings and booking setup
  • Tour manifests, checklists and run sheets
Rezdy ↗FareHarbor ↗TrekkSoft ↗Tourplan ↗
05 / GROUND TRANSPORT↗

Transfers, car rental & mobility

Ground mobility connects airports, hotels, attractions and destinations through transfers, chauffeur services, rental fleets, rail journeys and local transport information.

Freelance roles & services

  • Transfer and transport coordinator
  • Fleet / supplier data administrator
  • Route and journey researcher
  • Car rental content and listing specialist
  • Mobility customer support specialist
  • Transport integration QA tester

Typical deliverables

  • Transfer schedules and pickup instructions
  • Supplier and vehicle comparison tables
  • Route guides and arrival information
  • Booking and cancellation help articles
Rome2Rio ↗Google Maps ↗Amadeus ↗
06 / NICHE TRAVEL✳

Special-interest & managed travel

Specialist travel includes corporate travel, luxury and tailor-made trips, adventure and outdoor travel, accessible tourism, wellness, educational tours and group travel. Each niche can require its own supplier knowledge and service standards.

Freelance roles & services

  • Corporate travel coordinator
  • Luxury / tailor-made itinerary designer
  • Adventure and outdoor trip researcher
  • Accessible travel information researcher
  • Group travel and educational tour planner
  • Travel policy and expense workflow assistant

Typical deliverables

  • Traveler profiles and preference briefs
  • Accessibility and suitability information
  • Group rooming lists and travel packs
  • Travel policy guides and trip documentation
Travefy ↗Axus ↗Sabre ↗
07 / VISITOR ECONOMY◎

Attractions, museums, events & visitor experiences

Attractions and event operators manage admission, timed entry, group visits, visitor communications, capacity, ticket distribution and on-site experience.

Freelance roles & services

  • Ticketing and visitor-services coordinator
  • Attraction listing and destination content writer
  • Event and group booking assistant
  • Visitor journey / UX designer
  • Accessibility information editor
  • Experience review and feedback analyst

Typical deliverables

  • Ticketing FAQs and visitor information
  • Timed-entry and group-visit process guides
  • Event pages and experience descriptions
  • Visitor feedback summaries
FareHarbor ↗Rezdy ↗TrekkSoft ↗
08 / SERVICE & DIGITAL♡

Guest support, travel content & digital operations

This cross-cutting area supports the full visitor journey: destination discovery, pre-trip questions, in-trip assistance, reviews, post-trip communication, web content and marketing operations.

Freelance roles & services

  • Travel customer support / helpdesk agent
  • Destination researcher and travel writer
  • SEO content specialist for travel websites
  • CRM and guest lifecycle coordinator
  • Travel website content manager
  • Analytics and reputation reporting assistant

Typical deliverables

  • Destination guides, blogs and landing pages
  • FAQs, macros and customer support playbooks
  • CRM segments and pre/post-trip messages
  • Content calendars and performance reports
Zendesk ↗Intercom ↗WordPress ↗Google Analytics ↗Semrush ↗
