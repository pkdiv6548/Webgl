01 / DISTRIBUTION & SALES

Travel agencies & booking

Support agencies selling flights, hotels, packages and multi-supplier itineraries. Work involving ticket issuance, refunds or regulated seller obligations must stay with appropriately authorized staff.

Freelance roles

  • Travel itinerary planner
  • GDS / booking support specialist
  • Corporate travel operations assistant
  • Travel product and supplier researcher

Typical deliverables

  • Day-by-day itinerary and supplier comparison
  • Booking data QA checklist
  • Traveler preference and trip brief template
  • Fare / policy comparison with source notes
Tools to investigateAmadeusSabreTravelportTravefy
02 / AIRLINE OPERATIONS

Airlines & air travel

Airline work can involve complex operational, distribution and customer-service systems. Freelance scopes are often analytics, documentation, content, service-process or project-support tasks rather than direct operational control.

Freelance roles

  • Airline customer journey analyst
  • Ancillary product content specialist
  • Air travel disruption communications writer
  • Airline operations documentation assistant

Typical deliverables

  • Disruption message templates and escalation matrix
  • Ancillary / baggage policy content audit
  • Customer journey gap analysis
  • Operations SOP and training documents
Tools to investigateAmadeusSabreTravelportZendesk
03 / LODGING & REVENUE

Hotels & accommodation

Independent hotels, serviced apartments and multi-property groups need accurate room content, channel distribution, guest communications and reporting. Production changes require approval from the property owner or manager.

Freelance roles

  • Hotel listing and content auditor
  • Property management system implementation assistant
  • Channel distribution coordinator
  • Guest experience process designer

Typical deliverables

  • OTA listing consistency spreadsheet
  • Room / rate / amenity content matrix
  • Guest message templates and FAQ
  • Distribution issue log and handover guide
Tools to investigateCloudbedsSiteMinderMews
04 / EXPERIENCES & DESTINATIONS

Tour operators & DMC operations

Tour operators and destination management companies coordinate itineraries, suppliers, inventory, departures, guides and local services. Each deliverable should identify the system where approved information will be maintained.

Freelance roles

  • Tour operations coordinator
  • Activity inventory and booking specialist
  • Destination product researcher
  • Supplier contracting data assistant

Typical deliverables

  • Tour product setup sheet with inclusions and exclusions
  • Supplier contact and service matrix
  • Departure readiness checklist
  • Cancellation / change policy audit
Tools to investigateRezdyFareHarborTrekkSoftTourplan
05 / GROUND TRANSPORT

Transfers & mobility

Airport transfers, private drivers, rail connections and ground transport businesses need clear pickup instructions, reliable routing data and consistent supplier information. Live dispatch and safety decisions remain with authorized operators.

Freelance roles

  • Transfer itinerary coordinator
  • Route and connection research assistant
  • Ground transport supplier researcher
  • Mobility customer information writer

Typical deliverables

  • Pickup / drop-off instruction templates
  • Route options and connection-time comparison
  • Supplier service-level matrix
  • Accessibility and luggage information audit
Tools to investigateRome2RioGoogle MapsZendesk
06 / NICHE PRODUCTS

Special-interest travel

Adventure, wellness, culinary, accessible, educational and luxury travel products need subject-aware research and accurate expectations. Safety, accessibility and medical claims should be verified with qualified sources.

Freelance roles

  • Special-interest itinerary researcher
  • Accessible travel information auditor
  • Adventure tour product copywriter
  • Group trip logistics assistant

Typical deliverables

  • Audience-specific itinerary and packing guide
  • Accessibility information checklist
  • Activity suitability and requirements matrix
  • Group schedule, contact sheet and contingency plan
Tools to investigateTravefyAxus Travel AppWordPress
07 / VISITOR EXPERIENCES

Attractions, events & activities

Museums, venues, events and visitor attractions coordinate timed entry, ticket inventory, visitor instructions and post-visit feedback. Confirm the source of truth for capacity and dates before publishing.

Freelance roles

  • Attraction listing coordinator
  • Event travel logistics assistant
  • Ticketing content QA specialist
  • Visitor experience journey mapper

Typical deliverables

  • Listing and ticket information audit
  • Visitor arrival and entry guide
  • Event travel information pack
  • Post-visit survey and feedback summary
Tools to investigateFareHarborRezdyTrekkSoftIntercom
08 / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & GROWTH

Guest support & travel content

Travel businesses rely on accurate content and fast, consistent customer support before, during and after a trip. Access to customer records must be limited to the minimum needed and handled under the client’s privacy policies.

Freelance roles

  • Travel SEO content writer
  • Guest support knowledge-base editor
  • Travel CRM data quality assistant
  • Travel analytics and conversion analyst

Typical deliverables

  • Destination pages with fact-checking notes
  • Support macros and knowledge-base articles
  • CRM field hygiene and duplicate audit
  • Content performance report with recommendations
Tools to investigateWordPressSemrushGoogle AnalyticsZendeskIntercom
CLIENT BRIEF CHECKLIST

Four details that prevent rework.

01 · Business context

Business type, traveler segment, destination, season and current workflow.

02 · Access & systems

Exact tools, role permissions, data owner and whether a sandbox is available.

03 · Deliverable & acceptance

File format, quantity, sample output, reviewer and measurable completion criteria.

04 · Constraints & handover

Deadline, budget, data handling rules, approval steps and handover format.

Operational caution: do not share passwords in a brief. Use named accounts and least-privilege access. Never let an unqualified freelancer independently approve ticket issuance, refunds, revenue changes, safety-critical instructions or live inventory changes.
